## My Review
<b>Actual Star Rating: 3.5/5</b>

The title of this book is spot on and fits perfectly. It was truly not what I thought. This is the first book I have read by Clare Mackintosh. I did enjoy this book on the whole however, for me what started as a good psychological thriller eventually took a disaster turn. The story totally diverted into a completely different story making the whole plot feel disjointed. After the first twist was revealed, it felt like I was reading an entirely different book.


The book is written in 6 parts and is told through multiple POV. While I enjoyed certain parts, others felt dragged out, and the pacing was inconsistent. As for the twist they came in rapid succession toward the last 35% of the book, which made them feel a bit overwhelming rather than impactful. As for the characters, most of them were likeable.

With the current situation in the world, I do appreciate the author for addressing difficult themes like racism and radicalism

I love a good crime investigation thriller but I was looking for a more impactful domestic psychological thriller.

Thanks to Netgalley, Harpercollins and the Author Clare Mackintosh for this arc.
