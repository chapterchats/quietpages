In 1926, five women disappeared from a remote island in Vermont. Now, one hundred years later, it’s happening again.

Struggling actress Marin Keane is shocked when she lands a role in a major motion picture about the unsolved mystery of New Avalon, an island on sprawling Lake Faraday in Vermont. She’s even more surprised when she learns that the role requires a weeklong research trip to that very spot.

Because New Avalon isn’t your ordinary island. A century ago, it was a commune for spiritual mediums—until they all vanished in 1926. The only trace of them was five dresses hanging from the branches of an old oak tree in the middle of the island, one for each missing woman. Some locals say they simply left. Others think they were murdered. But the prevailing opinion, thanks to a diary left behind by one of the vanished, a young woman named Daisy Rue, is that a séance gone wrong conjured something supernatural that took them all one by one.

Not long after arriving, Marin and her castmates, including legendary actress Violet Wright and white-hot director Ronan Peters, begin to realize all is not right with New Avalon. They hear strange noises in the night and notice mysterious symbols left behind by the island's previous occupants. And after a sudden health emergency leaves Marin, Ronan, and the other actors stranded on the island, the disappearances begin again.

Is it the work of someone trying to derail the movie? Or is the island’s alleged supernatural past catching up with the present? As fear and suspicion mount, Marin turns to Daisy’s diary, hoping it holds the key to figuring out what really happened to the women of New Avalon—and how to keep the island’s terrible history from repeating itself.

## Settings:
The Unknownby Riley Sager is set on a remote, isolated island called New Avalon in Lake Faraday, Vermont. The setting has an eerie, atmospheric, haunting feel that's supposed to send a chill down your spine.

## Plot: 
A film is being made, and four famous actresses have been signed, along with one unknown, struggling actress who's cast in the lead role, a role she's unaware of until she's handed the script. The film is based on a haunting event that took place on this remote island 100 years ago, when five women went missing over the span of a week. The only trace left behind was five dresses hanging from an oak tree, the same dresses the women wore before they vanished from the island. Now the five actresses, along with the crew and director, are on set to spend a week on the island preparing before the final shoot begins in New York. The only question is whether the island is really haunted, or if someone is trying to derail the movie, or even seeking revenge.

## My thoughts:
This is the second book I've read by Riley Sager, and it left me with mixed feelings, much like the first. I do enjoy the plots of his books, but there are other aspects of his writing that never quite land for me, and The Unknown was no different.

The pacing for most of the book was slow, with all the major twists and revelations saved for the very end. What made it harder to follow was the large cast of characters combined with a repetitive storyline. Scenes from the diary entries are echoed again in the present-day timeline, which only complicates things further. At times, even keeping track of who was who became difficult.

For me personally, the horror didn't work. The setting was supposed to feel eerie and atmospheric, but I never felt it. It didn't give me a single chill. The horror elements felt oddly juvenile rather than genuinely unsettling. That said, the writing itself was easy to read, with a smooth flow, and the dual-timeline structure with diary entries was a nice touch even if the execution didn't fully work for me.

Overall, a decent read for those looking to explore horror as a new genre, or for readers who prefer something on the lighter side of the genre.
