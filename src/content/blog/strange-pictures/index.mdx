## My Review

<p>Like many Japanese literature reads, this one was heartwarming, quietly sad, and deeply thought-provoking. It tells the story of three people from different generations and the emotional journeys they carry through life.</p>

<p>I wasn’t expecting the leprosy angle in the story, but I appreciated learning more about the subject and how sensitively it was handled.</p>

<p>In terms of writing style, I did find parts a little repetitive, and surprisingly, it didn’t feel like a short read even though it is. That said, the characters were incredibly lovable and stayed with me.</p>

<p>Overall, a good and enjoyable read. 📖✨</p>
